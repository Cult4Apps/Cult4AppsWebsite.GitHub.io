Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 30, 2026

Our approach

Cult4Apps is a small independent studio. We build focused apps and try to keep our use of data as limited and understandable as possible.

Our apps

Our apps are designed to work without Cult4Apps collecting personal data from you. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers.

When you download our apps or make purchases through the App Store, information may be processed as part of providing those services. Cult4Apps does not receive your personal payment details.

If an app stores information on your device, that information is intended to stay on your device unless the app clearly offers a feature that sends or syncs it elsewhere.

Our website

Our website is used to present our apps and provide basic information. The website host may process standard technical logs, such as IP address, browser type, pages requested, and request time. These logs are used to keep the website running, reliable, and secure.

We do not use the website to sell personal data or build advertising profiles.

Future changes

If one of our apps needs to collect or share data in the future, we will update this page and the relevant App Store privacy information before making that change available.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted here.
